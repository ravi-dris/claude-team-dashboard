Key Insights
Adoption Health

AI is a daily work tool, not an experiment

Across four months the three teams have logged 28,854 prompts over 3,840 conversations — roughly 300+ prompts on most working days, weekends included. AI is embedded in how people work, not confined to office hours.

All 36 current seats are active — there are no idle seats. The one previously-dormant seat (Vishwa Patel) has been removed from Claude access, not left unused. All three team leads (Ravi, Ankit, Swapnil) remain active users, signalling sustained buy-in from the top down.
Usage Archetypes

Three distinct working styles have emerged

Deep collaborators — Kashish (23 msgs/session), Ravi (16), Akshat (12), Ankit·Services (11) — use AI for complex, hours-long problem solving.

Volume users — Aastha (338 convs), Sachin (293), Shweta (261), Banita (201), Krishna/ORG (158) — AI as always-on daily assistant.

Silent coders — Abhijit (464K, 6 chats), Navneet (261K), Ankit/ORG (138K, 5 chats), Ankit Yadav/ORG (127K, 4 chats), Aditya/ORG (116K, 1 chat) — minimal chat, massive code output.
Client Impact

AI is directly inside client deliverables

Real client work across all three teams is AI-assisted:

Product: NASSCOM portal testing, Bajaj Auto V2→V3, JSL dashboard, TRI NUO pipeline.
Services: CRISIL migration, Landmark patient vitals, ADG census, Aadhaar farmer workflow.
Dhwani ORG: Nasscom Foundation MIS on Frappe, LPR wireframes, Jansahas CMS design, architecture scalability assessments.
Productivity ROI

5.8M lines of code — and holding at scale

Combined code lines: 846K (Apr) → 1.66M (May) → 1.73M (Jun) → 1.57M (Jul, 3 wks) — nearly doubled from April, peaked in June, now sustaining ~1.6M/month. Product's 99.8% acceptance rate means virtually every AI-written line went to production.

At 30 lines/developer-hour, 5.81M lines ≈ 193,600 hours of equivalent manual coding — delivered in under 4 months by 34 active code contributors across 3 teams.
Team Comparison

Three teams, three strategies

Product — 1,556 convs, 1.91M code lines, 8.1 avg depth. AI for testing, SQL, data analysis, reporting.
Services — 975 convs, 2.44M code lines (most of any team). AI for migrations, DevOps, API integrations.
Dhwani ORG — 540 convs, 1.45M code lines. AI for product design (wireframes, MIS), backend builds, architecture planning. Krishna Maheshwari alone wrote 431K lines.
