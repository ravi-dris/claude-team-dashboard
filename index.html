Key Insights
Adoption Health

AI is a daily work tool, not an experiment

All three teams send 300+ prompts every working day. Even on weekends, conversations happen — showing AI has embedded into how people work, not just office hours.

32 of 35 members were active in the last 30 days. All three team leads (Ravi, Ankit, Swapnil) are active users, signalling cultural buy-in from the top down across the organisation.
Usage Archetypes

Three distinct working styles have emerged

Deep collaborators — Kashish (29 msgs/session), Pragat/ORG (10), Ravi (16), Vivek/ORG (12.7) — use AI for complex, hours-long problem solving.

Volume users — Aastha (302 convs), Sachin (257), Krishna/ORG (117), Swapnil/ORG (50) — AI as always-on daily assistant.

Silent coders — Abhijit (165K, 3 chats), Ankit/ORG (77K, 1 chat), Aditya/ORG (60K, 1 chat) — minimal chat, massive code output.
Client Impact

AI is directly inside client deliverables

Real client work across all three teams is AI-assisted:

Product: NASSCOM portal testing, Bajaj Auto V2→V3, JSL dashboard, TRI NUO pipeline.
Services: CRISIL migration, Landmark patient vitals, ADG census, Aadhaar farmer workflow.
Dhwani ORG: Nasscom Foundation MIS on Frappe, LPR wireframes, Jansahas CMS design, architecture scalability assessments.
Productivity ROI

Code grew 96% — all three teams combined

Combined code lines (all teams): 845K (April) → 1.66M (May) — a 96% surge. Product's 99.8% acceptance rate means virtually every AI-written line went straight to production.

At 30 lines/developer-hour, 2.65M lines = ~88,400 hours of equivalent manual coding — delivered in 63 days by 27 active code contributors across 3 teams.

Dhwani ORG — 302 convs, 594K code lines. AI for product design (wireframes, MIS), backend builds, architecture planning. Krishna Maheshwari alone wrote 231K lines.
