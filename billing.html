Key Insights

✦ Dhwani ORG: Highest Spend, Lowest Activity

Dhwani ORG accounts for $5,164 (47% of all spend), yet only has 302 conversations and 594K code lines — the lowest activity of the three teams. Billing only runs through April (no May/June invoices). At $17/conversation, cost-per-use is the highest — likely driven by annual seat charges billed upfront.

✦ Product Team: Best Value per Conversation

Product spends $3,057 for 1,189 conversations = $2.57 per conversation — best ROI of the three teams. May jumped to $1,113 with a new member add. June billing ($1,150) is consistent with May, suggesting stable seat count and usage going forward.

✦ Services: Growing Fast

Services billing has grown every month — April $655 → May $998 → June $1,175 (+79% in two months). 9 members were added in May ($225 seat invoice). Two May invoices are Partially Refunded ($87.45 + $113.96) — worth checking what triggered those credits.

✦ Combined: $2.57 avg cost per conversation

Across all 3 teams, total Apr–Jun spend was $8,628 (excluding March) for 2,122 conversations = $4.07 per conversation. Per code line: 2.06M lines for ~$8K = $0.004 per AI-written line of code — a strong productivity ROI.

✦ Seat Costs vs Usage Costs

Seat invoices (with member count) total approximately $5,975 across all accounts — 54% of all spend. Usage/overage invoices total ~$5,074. This means recurring seat costs are the larger driver. Adding members drives the biggest billing jumps.

✦ April Was the Most Expensive Month

April 2026 combined (all 3 accounts): $4,192 — the highest single month, driven by Dhwani ORG's $2,743 April billing. May dropped to $2,111 (ORG had no billing). June is on track for ~$2,325. If Dhwani ORG has no further invoices, monthly spend is stabilising around $2,000–$2,300/month.
