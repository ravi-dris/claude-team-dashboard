Key Insights

✦ Strong Improvement Month-on-Month

Total general days fell from 243.3 (April) to 151.9 (May) — a 37.5% reduction. This means 91 fewer non-billable days in May, signalling better project allocation or improved discipline in timesheet submissions.

✦ Bench Time Nearly Eliminated

Bench hours dropped from 23.4 days (April) to just 0.75 days (May) — a 96.8% reduction. This is the strongest positive signal: idle employees have been deployed to projects. Bench time should remain near zero going forward.

✦ Meetings = 63% of General Time

Discussions / Meeting and Internal Discussions/Meetings together account for ~63% of all general hours in both months. While some meetings are necessary, this proportion warrants reviewing which recurring meetings can be reduced or made more efficient.

✦ Several Moved to Billable (Positive)

Employees like Shubhiksha Khandelwal (21.4 → 0), Osama Islam (14.6 → 0), Shlok (14 → 0), Priyambada Pandey (13.4 → 0) and Rekha Ghangal (10.2 → 0) had high April general hours but zero in May — very likely moved to billable projects.
