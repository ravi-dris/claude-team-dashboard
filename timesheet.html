Key Insights

✦ June Improved — Down 11%

June general hours fell to 130.3 days from 146.3 (May) and 144.7 (April) — the first meaningful reduction. Non-billable time is trending down, a positive sign that more people moved onto billable project work in June.

✦ Bench Time Jumped — Watch This

Bench hours rose sharply to 11.1 days in June, up from just 0.75 (May) and 1.1 (April). This is the one metric moving the wrong way — it signals some people were between projects. Worth confirming these are now re-allocated to billable work.

✦ Meetings Share Dropped to 53%

Discussions and internal meetings fell to ~53% of general hours in June (69.0 days) from ~66% in Apr/May. A healthy shift — less time in meetings relative to other internal work. Still the largest single category, so continued review of recurring meetings remains worthwhile.

✦ Persistent High Submitters

Kandhi Omprakash (16.1 May → 21.0 Jun) and Aditi Baitule Deshpande (18.9 → 16.6) remain the top general-hour submitters. Both are senior roles logging heavy POC/meeting time — a review of their project allocation is recommended.

✦ Sharp June Spikes — Needs Review

Bhushan Barbuddhe jumped from 1.2 → 12.4 days and Brahmjyot Singh (0.2 → 5.1) and new joiner Diksha Hazarika (5.2) also spiked. Confirm their assignment status and ensure they're allocated to billable work.

✦ POC Work Steady

Software - POC held at 30.4 days in June (vs 33.4 in May, 23.4 in April) — sustained prototyping and exploration. If these POCs convert to billable projects, it will show up positively in the coming months' general hours.
